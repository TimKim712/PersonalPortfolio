About Me

Thanks for visiting! I'm a recent graduate from San José State University with a B.S in software engineering. Currently, I am an Academic Graduate Appointee at the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory for the Infrastructure Applications Programming team; I primarily work on a management tool that helps support scheduling, planning, executing, and tracking various infrastructure projects. My main interests lie in software development, but I also love to dabble into machine learning, distributed systems, site reliability, and low-level systems. Overall, I'm always exploring other areas that spark my interest and where my skills can be best applied!

During my undergraduate studies, I was the treasurer of our school's ACM chapter and also competed in the university's archery team. Outside of school, you can catch me playing basketball and video games, or just having a chill time with family and friends. If anything here sparks your interest, please don't hesitate in contacting me!
